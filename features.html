Features

Built for batch-level quality control

Replaces slow manual counting with a repeatable result and a record buyers can check.

Image-based counting

Detects suspected particles in images of the residue and filtrate, with an adjustable darkness threshold.

Image Detection

Particle classification

Sorts detections into fibers, fragments and beads by shape, so each batch shows what kind of plastic it holds.

Image Detection

Closed-loop filtration

Repeats up to three passes when the batch is over the limit, then flags it for manual review.

Filtration Monitoring

Salt recovery

Purified filtrate is recrystallized into usable salt instead of discarding a failed batch.

Results and Reports

Batch reports

Before-and-after counts, removal efficiency and filtration history, exported as PDF or CSV.

Results and Reports

Validation against manual counts

Compares automated counts with manual microscope counts, with error and correlation, so the method can be defended.
