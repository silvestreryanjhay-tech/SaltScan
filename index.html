Built for batch-level quality control

Replaces slow manual counting with a repeatable result and a record buyers can check.

Image-based counting

Detects and classifies fibers, fragments and beads from images of the residue and filtrate.

Closed-loop filtration

Repeats up to three passes when the batch is over the limit, then flags it for review.

Salt recovery

Purified filtrate is recrystallized into usable salt instead of discarding a failed batch.
